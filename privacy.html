Your Privacy Matters

This Privacy Policy explains how the Julio Guzman for Mayor of Homestead campaign ("Campaign," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you visit julioformayor.com (including visitors redirected from votejulioguzman.com), sign our supporter pledge, volunteer, or contact us.

Privacy contact: info@julioformayor.com  |  +1 (786) 229-0157

Information We Collect, and Why

Supporter pledge (pledge.html). When you sign the pledge we collect:

  • Name, email address, and mobile number, to record your pledge, avoid duplicate entries, and contact you only through the channels you agree to.
  • Home address, plus the map location (latitude/longitude) and ZIP code taken from the address you choose, to check whether you live inside Homestead city limits and to organize neighborhood outreach.
  • Voting information you share: the number of registered voters in your household, whether you are registered at that address, and how you plan to vote, so we can send relevant reminders (for example, when to request a mail ballot).
  • Optional answers: the issues that matter most to you, ways you would like to help, your preferred language, and your preferred contact method, so we can follow up appropriately.
  • Your typed name confirming the pledge, to show the pledge was made by you.
  • Your contact permissions: whether you agreed to email, text messages, and/or phone calls, together with the exact wording you saw, its version, the language, and the date and time, so we keep an accurate record of what you agreed to.
  • Referral and source codes in the link you used (for example, a friend's share code or an event code), to understand which outreach is working. After you sign, you receive a random share code that does not contain your name or phone number.

The pledge is a statement of support. It is not an official candidate petition, voter registration, or ballot request.

Public voter records. We may compare pledge information with publicly available voter registration and voting-history records from the Miami-Dade County Supervisor of Elections to confirm registration status and plan outreach. We do not publish the results.

Volunteer and contact forms (volunteer.html, contact.html). These forms open your own email app with your details filled in. Nothing is sent until you press Send, and the information (name, email, phone, ZIP code, how you'd like to help, your message, and any contact permissions you choose) reaches us only as an email to info@julioformayor.com. We use it to respond and to coordinate volunteer activity.

Contributions. This website does not process donations online. If you contribute, we collect the information Florida campaign finance law requires, such as your name, address, occupation, and the amount, and report it as described below.

Information collected automatically. We do not use advertising trackers, analytics tools, or tracking pixels on this site. Our website host (GitHub Pages) and the services that deliver the map and form features may record standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, under their own privacy policies. Your browser stores your language choice (English, Español, Kreyòl) locally on your device; we do not receive it unless you submit a form.

Contact Permissions: Email, Text, and Phone

Email, text messages, and phone calls are separate permissions. Each box on our forms is unchecked unless you check it, and none is required to sign the pledge or volunteer. We contact you only through the channels you agreed to, except to respond to a message you sent us.

  • Email: use the unsubscribe link in any campaign email, or email info@julioformayor.com.
  • Text messages: reply STOP to any campaign text to stop receiving them, or HELP for help. Message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply.
  • Phone calls: tell the caller you do not want further calls, or contact us at the email or phone number above.

Opting out of one channel does not opt you out of the others unless you ask. We keep a record of your opt-out so that we continue to honor it. Mobile numbers and text-message consent are not shared with third parties for their own marketing.

How We Store and Protect Information

  • Pledge information is stored in a secured database hosted by our database provider, Supabase. Data is encrypted in transit between your browser and the database.
  • The website's public key can only submit a pledge and read the total supporter count; it cannot read anyone's pledge.
  • Only authorized campaign staff with individual accounts can view supporter records, and only for campaign purposes.
  • The pledge form does not reveal whether an email address or phone number is already on file, and a later submission cannot change the name, email, phone number, or address on an existing pledge. Corrections are made by staff on request.
  • The record of contact permissions is kept as a log that campaign staff cannot edit or delete.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and limit who can access it.

Service Providers and Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it only as follows:

  • Service providers who operate the tools we use, and only as needed to provide those services: Supabase (pledge database), Google Maps Platform (address suggestions and the city-limits map; the address you type is sent to Google), GitHub Pages (website hosting), jsDelivr (delivers software code used by the pledge form), and our email provider. If we use a text-messaging or calling service, it will receive only the contact details needed to reach people who agreed to that channel.
  • As required by law, including Florida campaign finance reporting, or in response to a valid legal request.

Florida Campaign Finance Disclosure

Campaign contributions are reported to the appropriate filing officer as required by Florida law. These reports are public records and may include contributor names, addresses, occupations, contribution amounts, and other information required by law.

How Long We Keep Information

  • Pledge, volunteer, and contact information: kept for the 2027 Homestead municipal election cycle and deleted or de-identified within 12 months after the campaign's final election, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.
  • Contact permission and opt-out records: kept as long as we hold your contact information, and opt-out records for as long as needed to keep honoring your choice.
  • Contribution records: kept for the period required by Florida campaign finance law.

Your Choices: Access, Correction, and Deletion

  • Access or correction: email info@julioformayor.com to ask what pledge information we hold about you or to correct it.
  • Deletion: email info@julioformayor.com to ask us to delete your pledge and contact information. We will confirm the request comes from you (for example, by replying to the email address on file) and complete it within 30 days. We may keep a minimal record of your opt-out so we do not contact you again, and any information we are legally required to keep, such as contribution records.
  • Withdrawing contact permission: see "Contact Permissions" above. You can withdraw any permission at any time.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last Updated" date. If we change how we use contact information you already gave us, we will ask for your permission again where required.
