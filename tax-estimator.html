Amendment 3 · Homeowner Tool

2027 Property Tax Estimator

Florida voters approved Amendment 3 on November 3, 2026. Starting with the 2027 tax bill, it raises the homestead exemption on non-school property taxes. Use this tool to get a rough idea of what it could mean for your home in Homestead.

1. Find your assessed value

Search your address on the Miami-Dade Property Appraiser site. Look for 2026 Assessed Value (not Market Value).

Your address is not saved or sent to the campaign. It only opens the Property Appraiser search in a new tab.

2027 and 2028 rows use today's 2026 tax rates. The real rates for 2027 will be set by the City, County, School Board and other taxing bodies in September 2027 and may go up or down. Garbage, stormwater and other flat fees on your bill are not included.

How this estimate works

  • Non-school exemption. Today a homestead gets up to $51,411 off its value for non-school taxes. Under Amendment 3 that becomes $150,000 in 2027 and $250,000 in 2028, then rises with inflation starting in 2029. The new amount replaces the old one; it is not added on top.
  • School taxes do not change. School taxes keep the existing $25,000 exemption.
  • New Floridians. If Florida was not your permanent home by December 31, 2026, the non-school exemption is $50,000 for your first four years, then the full amount.
  • Save Our Homes still limits how much a homestead's assessed value can rise each year (3% or inflation, whichever is lower). For non-homestead property, the yearly cap on non-school taxes drops from 10% to 5% starting in 2027.
  • Other exemptions (senior, veteran, disability, widow/widower) are not included. If you have one, your bill may be lower.
  • Tax rates used (per $1,000 of taxable value): City of Homestead (adopted September 2026), Miami-Dade County, Fire-Rescue, Library and other non-school levies , and School Board . County and school figures are the proposed 2026 rates published by the Property Appraiser in August 2026. Your TRIM notice or tax bill shows the exact rates for your parcel.

This is an estimate for general information, not tax advice. For your official values and exemptions, contact the Miami-Dade Property Appraiser. Questions about this tool: info@julioformayor.com.
